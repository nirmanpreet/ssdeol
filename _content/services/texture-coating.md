## Why texture coating is worth it at this scale

Hand-rendering a large commercial elevation is slow, weather-dependent, and the
finish varies with whoever is holding the float that day. Sprayed texture
coating is faster to apply, far more consistent, and — properly specified —
lasts considerably longer.

It suits:

- Large concrete and panel elevations
- Elevations over existing render that needs renewing
- Buildings where appearance has drifted but the fabric is sound

It does not suit a wall that is wet, unstable or structurally compromised. That
needs repairing first.

## How we approach it

1. **Full inspection and testing.** We tap-test the existing render, map the
   areas that have blown off, and identify where water is getting in.
2. **Strip and repair.** Loose and failed render comes off. Cracks get opened
   and properly repaired, not filled over.
3. **Substrate repair.** Panel joints, flashings and concrete defects are made
   good. No coating fixes a moving joint.
4. **Prime.** Selected for the substrate — different render and different
   masonry need different primers.
5. **Spray the finish.** Even coverage at a controlled thickness, which is what
   gives the consistent appearance.
6. **Protect and clean down.** Windows, cladding, paving and planting covered
   and cleaned as we go.

## Colour and appearance

Commercial buildings often need to stay visually consistent with a corporate
palette or an adjoining section that is not being coated. We can colour-match to
an existing finish so the renewal is not obvious from the road — which is
usually the whole point of doing it.

## Access

Access is usually the largest single line in a texture coating quote. We will
tell you honestly whether scaffold, a boom lift or rope access is the right call
for the building, and where a cheaper system would do the job just as well.
